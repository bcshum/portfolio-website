Hello, I'm Bryan Shum.

Cognitive Science × Interactive Arts & Technology.

I am a Cognitive Science and Interactive Arts and Technology student. I am an interaction and graphic designer who is passionate about the intersection between human behaviour and digital technology. I believe that by understanding how people perceive, process, and interact with information, I can create interfaces that are visually compelling and cognitively intuitive to use by merging research-based design methods with expressive visual elements.

My technical skills include proficiency in design software such as Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign and Figma, as well as prototyping software including ProtoPie, UXPin, Balsamiq and animation/modelling software Autodesk Maya.
